Determine scope, stakeholders, and analysis approach.

Gather data from surveys, databases, APIs, or experiments.

6. Modeling

Apply statistical or machine learning methods.

7. Interpretation

Translate results into meaningful insights.

8. Reporting

Communicate findings through visuals and reports.

9. Decision-Making

Drive actions using data-driven insights.

10. Deployment

Implement and monitor solutions over time.
