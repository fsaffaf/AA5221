Data Analytics Process

1. Define the Research Problem

Identify objectives and key questions.

2. Requirements & Planning

Define scope, stakeholders, and success metrics.

3. Data Collection

Gather data from relevant sources.

4. Data Preparation

Clean and transform your data.

5. Exploratory Analysis

Discover patterns and trends.

6. Modeling

Apply statistical or ML techniques.

7. Interpretation

Turn results into insights.

8. Reporting

Communicate findings effectively.

9. Decision-Making

Support business or research actions.

10. Deployment

Implement and monitor solutions.
