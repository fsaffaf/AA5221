Data Analytics Process

1. Define the Research Problem

Clearly define objectives, questions, and expected outcomes.

2. Understand Requirements & Plan

Determine scope, data needs, stakeholders, and success criteria.

3. Data Collection

Gather relevant data from surveys, databases, or external sources.

4. Data Preparation

Clean, transform, and organize the dataset for analysis.

5. Exploratory Data Analysis

Identify patterns, trends, and anomalies using statistics and visuals.

6. Modeling / Analysis

Apply statistical or machine learning methods to extract insights.

7. Interpretation

Translate results into meaningful conclusions and implications.

8. Reporting & Visualization

Communicate findings through reports, dashboards, and visuals.

9. Decision-Making

Use insights to support actions and recommendations.

10. Deployment & Monitoring

Implement solutions and monitor performance over time.
