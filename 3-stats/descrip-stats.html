Lecture 2

Types of Data

OVERVIEW

In research, data refers to the information that researchers collect in order to answer questions. The type of data you collect determines how you analyze it and what conclusions you can draw. There are two primary types of data: quantitative and qualitative. Understanding the difference between these is essential for choosing the correct research methods.

Quantitative data is numerical data that can be analyzed mathematically. It allows researchers to calculate averages, compare groups, and perform statistical tests.
Qualitative data is descriptive, non-numerical data that focuses on understanding meaning, experiences, and perspectives.

Many studies combine both types of data to gain a more complete understanding of a research question.
