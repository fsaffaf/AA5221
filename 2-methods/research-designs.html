Scientific Research

Research Designs

There are three main study types. Think of them as a spectrum of strength for proving causation.

Strongest → Weakest: True Experiment → Quasi → Correlational

STRONGEST True Experiment

A true experiment allows you to claim causality (X causes Y).

Must include ALL 3:
  • Manipulation
  • Random Assignment
  • High Control

1. Manipulation

You create groups (e.g., high vs low dose).

2. Random Assignment

A computer randomly places participants into groups → removes bias.

3. Control

Everything is kept the same except the independent variable.

---

MEDIUM Quasi-Experiment

"Quasi" = almost.

Includes only 1 or 2 of the 3 requirements → cannot prove causation.

Why use it?

  • Ethics (can't assign harmful conditions)
  • Practical limits (time, money)
---

LOWEST Correlational

Measures relationships ONLY.

❌ No manipulation ❌ No random assignment ❌ No control ➡️ No causation

Still useful for early research and identifying patterns.

Between vs Within Designs

Between-Subjects

Each participant = ONE condition only
  • ✅ Faster
  • ✅ No carryover effects
  • ❌ Needs more participants
---

Within-Subjects

Each participant = ALL conditions
  • ✅ Fewer participants needed
  • ✅ More efficient data
  • ❌ Takes longer
  • ❌ Risk of carryover effects
---
