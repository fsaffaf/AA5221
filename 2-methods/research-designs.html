Scientific Research

Research Designs

Think of these as a spectrum of how strong your conclusions are.

Strongest → Weakest: True Experiment → Quasi → Correlational

STRONGEST True Experiment

Allows you to claim causation.

Must have:
  • Manipulation
  • Random Assignment
  • Control

MEDIUM Quasi

“Almost” an experiment.

Missing at least one key requirement → no causation

LOW Correlational

No manipulation, no random assignment, no control → relationship only

Between vs Within

Between-Subjects

One condition per participant
  • Faster
  • No carryover
  • Needs more participants

Within-Subjects

Participants experience all conditions
  • Fewer participants
  • More data efficient
  • Carryover risk
Between Within
One condition All conditions
More participants Fewer participants
No carryover Carryover possible
