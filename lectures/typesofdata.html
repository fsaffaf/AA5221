Types of Data

Overview

Understanding levels of measurement is one of the most important skills in statistics and analytics. The level of measurement determines which statistical techniques, charts, and analyses are appropriate for your data.

Levels of Measurement

Levels of measurement are ...

Level Definition Examples Data Type
Nominal Categories with no rank order. Race, gender, nationality Categorical
Ordinal Categories can be ranked, but the distance between categories is unknown. Single-item Likert scales Categorical
Interval Equal spacing between values, but no true zero. Temperature (°F, °C), composite Likert scales Continuous
Ratio Equal spacing between values and a meaningful zero point. Weight, height, age, income, distance Continuous
