Technology Guide

Microsoft OneDrive

Overview

In this course, Microsoft OneDrive is used to organize coding assignment files and provide access to submitted work.

What is Microsoft OneDrive?

Microsoft OneDrive is the cloud storage service provided by Microsoft. It allows you to store files online, access them from multiple devices, and share documents with others.

Important: Do NOT use Google Drive for course submissions. Google Drive is a completely separate cloud storage system. All course files should be stored and shared through Microsoft OneDrive.

Why is Digital File Organization Important?

Effective file organization is an essential skill in both academic and professional settings. Organizing files into clearly labeled folders helps you quickly locate documents, prevent lost work, and maintain a logical structure for projects and assignments.

File organization becomes especially important when working with multiple versions of documents, submitting assignments, or collaborating with others.

Professional File Organization Practices

  • Create folders with clear and descriptive names.
  • Keep related files together in the same location.
  • Avoid storing important course files only on your personal computer.
  • Use consistent naming conventions for assignments and projects.
  • Share files using appropriate permissions.
  • Use shared cloud storage to provide access to the most current version of a file without repeatedly sending email attachments.

What is Cloud Storage?

Simple Definition: There are two primary ways files can be stored: local storage and cloud storage.
Topic Local Storage Cloud Storage
Where are files stored? On your computer, USB drive, flash drive, or external hard drive. On secure internet-based servers accessed through your account.
How do you access them? You access files directly from the device where they are stored. You can access files from multiple devices by signing in and connecting to the internet.
What happens if your device is damaged, lost, or stolen? You may permanently lose access to your files. Your files remain available because they are stored separately from your device.
Think of it like... Keeping important documents in your home. If something happens to your home, your documents may be lost. Keeping important documents in a secure bank vault. You can still access them even if something happens to your home.
Example Saving your R files only on your personal computer. Saving your R files to Microsoft OneDrive.
Final Point Main limitation: Your files depend on the physical device where they are stored. Main advantage: Your files remain accessible even when your personal device is unavailable.

Key Takeaway

Microsoft OneDrive helps protect your work by storing files in the cloud, making them accessible from multiple devices while providing a consistent location for organizing and sharing course materials.

Developing good file organization habits now will make coursework easier and prepare you for professional environments where cloud storage and digital collaboration are standard practice.
