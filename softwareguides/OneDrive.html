Microsoft OneDrive

Overview

Effective file organization is an essential skill for professionals. Organizing files into clearly labeled folders helps you quickly locate documents, prevent lost work, and maintain a logical structure for projects and assignments. File organization becomes especially important when working with multiple versions of documents, submitting assignments, or collaborating with others.

Modern professionals should be able to:

  • Create folders with clear and descriptive names.
  • Keep related files together in the same location.
  • Avoid storing important course files only on your personal computer.
  • Use consistent naming conventions for assignments and projects.
  • Share files using appropriate permissions.
  • Use shared cloud storage to share the most current version of a file without repeatedly sending email attachments.

Modern professionals should also primarily use cloud storage to store their computer documents. More and more companies require employees to keep documents on secure cloud servers and are not allowed to store them on personal devices.

Local Storage vs Cloud Storage

Topic Local Storage Cloud Storage
Where are files stored? On your computer, USB drive, flash drive, or external hard drive. On secure internet-based servers.
How do you access them? Directly from the device where they are stored. From multiple devices by signing in and connecting to the internet.
If the device is lost or damaged You may lose access to your files. Your files remain available online.
Think of it Like... Keeping important documents in your home. Keeping important documents in a secure bank vault.
Example Saving your R files only on your computer. Saving your R files to Microsoft OneDrive.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Create and Share a Folder in Microsoft OneDrive
Step 1: Open Microsoft OneDrive

OneDrive is Microsoft's online file storage system. It allows you to save files online and share them with other people.

  1. Open your internet browser. This is the program you use to visit websites, such as Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. Click the address bar at the very top of the browser window. Type www.microsoft365.com and press the Enter key on your keyboard.
  3. Select Sign in if prompted.
  4. Enter your SLU Microsoft 365 email address and password. Follow any additional sign-in instructions if requested.
  5. Once you are signed in, look for OneDrive. You may need to select the app launcher (the icon made of small squares) or look through the available Microsoft apps.
  6. Select OneDrive to open it.
What should you see?
You should see a page containing your files and folders. A folder is similar to a folder on your computer: it helps you organize related files in one place.
Having trouble signing in?
Make sure you are using your SLU account rather than a personal Microsoft account. If you cannot access your SLU account, contact SLU Information Technology for assistance.
Step 2: Create a New Folder

You will now create a folder specifically for your assignment.

  1. Make sure you are on your main OneDrive page. If you have opened another folder, use the navigation or breadcrumb links near the top to return to your main file list.
  2. Look near the top of the page for a button labeled + New or New.
  3. Click New. A menu should appear.
  4. Select Folder from the menu.
  5. A small window should appear asking you to name your folder. Click in the name box.
  6. Type the following folder name exactly as shown:
Example Folder Name:
AA 5221 Assignment 5
  1. Click Create.
  2. Look for your new folder in the list of files and folders. You may need to wait a moment or refresh the page.
Important:
Use a folder name that clearly identifies your assignment. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in the folder name.
What if you do not see the same buttons?
The OneDrive screen may look slightly different depending on your browser, account, or Microsoft updates. Look for a button labeled New or an option to create a folder.
Step 3: Open Your New Folder
  1. Find the folder you just created.
  2. Move your mouse pointer over the folder name.
  3. Double-click the folder name. To double-click, press the left mouse button twice quickly.
  4. Wait for the folder to open.
What should you see?
The folder should open to a page where you can store your assignment files. If it is a new folder, the page may be empty.
Remember:
Opening a folder is different from selecting it. If you only select the folder, you may need to double-click its name to open it.
Step 4: Add Your Assignment Files to the Folder

You can upload files from your computer into OneDrive. Uploading means copying a file from your computer to your online OneDrive storage.

  1. Make sure you have opened the correct assignment folder.
  2. Look near the top of the page for + Add new, Upload, or a similar option. The wording may vary.
  3. Select Upload, then choose Files if a menu appears.
  4. A window will open showing files and folders on your computer. This is called the file selection window.
  5. Find the location where your assignment files are saved. For example, they may be in your Documents folder, on your Desktop, or in another folder you created.
  6. Click the file you want to upload. If you need to upload more than one file at once, hold down the Ctrl key while clicking each file on a Windows computer.
  7. After selecting your file or files, click Open in the file selection window.
  8. Wait for OneDrive to finish uploading. Keep the browser window open while the files are uploading.
  9. Check that your files appear in the folder.
Example:
Your AA 5221 Assignment 5 folder might contain your R script, data file, and HTML report, depending on the files required for the assignment.
Check before continuing:
Make sure your files are inside the assignment folder, not just somewhere else in OneDrive. Open the folder and verify that you can see the file names.
Do not see your file?
It may be saved in a different location on your computer. In the file selection window, check Documents, Desktop, Downloads, or the folder where you saved your assignment.
Step 5: Share the Folder with Your Instructor

Sharing gives another person permission to access your folder. You should share the entire assignment folder rather than sharing each file separately.

  1. Return to the main OneDrive file list or the location where your assignment folder appears.
  2. Find your assignment folder. Do not open the folder for this step; you need to select the folder itself.
  3. Move your mouse pointer over the folder name. Look for a Share button or icon. You may need to click the three dots (...) next to the folder to find the option.
  4. Select Share. A sharing window should appear.
  5. Look for the sharing settings. Depending on your account, you may see an option such as People you choose, People in Saint Louis University, or another link setting.
  6. Choose the option that allows your instructor to access the folder. If you are sharing directly with your instructor, enter the instructor's SLU email address in the recipient box.
  7. Check the permission setting. If your instructor only needs to view and download your work, Can view may be sufficient. If your instructor needs to edit files in the folder, use Can edit only when instructed to do so.
  8. If you see a Send button, select it to send the sharing invitation. If you are creating a link instead, follow the next step.
What is a permission?
A permission determines what another person can do with your files.
  • Can view: The person can open and view files but generally cannot edit them.
  • Can edit: The person may be able to change files or add and delete items in the shared folder.
Important:
Do not assume that a folder is shared just because you created it. You must complete the sharing process and make sure your instructor has access. Avoid giving editing permission unless it is required.
Step 6: Copy a Link to Your Shared Folder

If your assignment instructions ask you to submit a OneDrive link, follow these steps.

  1. Find your assignment folder in OneDrive.
  2. Select the folder or click the three dots (...) beside its name.
  3. Select Share.
  4. Look for Copy link. If you do not see it immediately, check the sharing window for additional options.
  5. Before copying the link, review the sharing settings. Make sure the link allows the intended recipient to access the folder. If the link settings are editable, select the appropriate option and apply the changes.
  6. Select Copy or Copy link, depending on the option displayed. OneDrive should copy the link to your computer's clipboard.
  7. Return to your assignment submission page in Canvas.
  8. Click in the area where you are asked to submit your link.
  9. Hold down the Ctrl key and press V on your keyboard. This pastes the copied link into the submission area.
  10. Review your submission to make sure the link appears correctly. Follow the assignment instructions to submit your work.
What does copying a link mean?
Copying a link saves the web address temporarily so you can paste it somewhere else. You do not need to memorize or retype the link.
Helpful keyboard shortcuts:
  • Ctrl + C: Copy selected text or a link.
  • Ctrl + V: Paste copied text or a link.
On a Mac, use the Command key instead of Ctrl.
Important:
Copying a link does not automatically give everyone permission to open it. Your instructor must have access through the link settings or through the sharing invitation you sent.
Step 7: Test Your Shared Folder Link

Before submitting your assignment, check that your folder contains the correct files and that the sharing settings are appropriate.

  1. Open your assignment folder in OneDrive.
  2. Check that all required files are present and that their names are correct.
  3. Return to the folder's sharing settings and confirm that your instructor has been given access.
  4. If you submitted a link in Canvas, check that you copied the complete link and submitted it in the correct location.
  5. If you want to test the link, open a private or incognito browser window and paste the link into the address bar.
  6. If prompted to sign in, use an account that has permission to access the folder. A private browser window may not be signed in to your SLU account.
  7. Confirm that you can open the folder and see the expected files.
A link that works for you may not work for your instructor.
Your browser may already be signed in to your SLU account. A successful test does not guarantee that another person has permission. Confirm the sharing settings and make sure your instructor has been granted access.
Step 8: Troubleshooting Common Problems

If something does not work as expected, review the suggestions below.

Problem: I cannot find OneDrive.

  • Go to Microsoft 365 and sign in with your SLU account.
  • Look for OneDrive among the available Microsoft apps.

Problem: I cannot find the New Folder option.

  • Return to your main OneDrive file list.
  • Look for New or + Add new near the top of the page.

Problem: I cannot find my assignment file.

  • Check Documents, Desktop, Downloads, and any folder where you may have saved the file.
  • Make sure you are looking in the correct location on your computer.

Problem: My instructor cannot open the link.

  • Open the sharing settings for your folder.
  • Confirm that you shared the folder with the correct SLU email address or created a link that permits the intended recipient to access it.
  • Check that the link was copied completely and submitted correctly in Canvas.

Problem: I shared a file, but not the folder.

  • Return to your main OneDrive file list.
  • Find the assignment folder itself and repeat the sharing steps for that folder.
